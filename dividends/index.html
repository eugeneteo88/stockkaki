+What is a good dividend yield in Singapore?
Roughly 4–6% is a solid, sustainable yield for a Singapore dividend stock or REIT. Much higher — say above 10% — is worth a closer look, as it may include a special dividend or signal elevated risk.
+Are dividends taxed in Singapore?
No. Singapore uses a one-tier corporate tax system, so dividends paid to individual shareholders are tax-free, and there is no capital-gains tax.
+How do I buy dividend stocks in Singapore?
Through any SGX brokerage (DBS Vickers, moomoo, Tiger, Interactive Brokers and others) or with SRS funds. You must own the shares before the ex-dividend date to receive the next payout.
